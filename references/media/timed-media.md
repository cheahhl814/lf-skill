# Medium adapter — timed media (videos, podcasts)

Loaded by `SKILL.md` Phase 0 when the study target is audio or video. Loop, integrity
rules and ledger are unchanged. Anchors are timestamps.

## Intake

Requires a **timestamped transcript**. For YouTube, use the external `youtube-extract`
skill (declared in `skills:`); for other sources, a captions file or transcript works. lf-skill does not extract media itself; if
no transcript is available, ask the student for one and stop. Questions must quote the
transcript and cite the timestamp (`[12:40]`) so the ledger and instructor can find the
moment.

## Objectives (derivation fallback)

- **Main claims** — the 3–5 points the speaker is making.
- **Method / demonstration** — any procedure or analysis shown, and why each step.
- **Numbers and terms** — figures, thresholds and jargon the student must explain.
- **Limits** — what the speaker assumes, skips, or hedges.

Tiering: claims and demonstrated steps are must-know; asides and anecdotes are
good-to-know.

## Anchors (checkpoints)

- Chapter or topic boundaries from the transcript; otherwise every ~10 minutes.
- Max 6 per session. The student pauses at the timestamp you name, answers, then continues.
- For passive listening (podcasts), prefer one prediction before a segment and one
  recall + apply after it.

## Evidence of understanding

- **Predict:** "at [T] the speaker is about to show X — what do you expect and why?"
- **Recall in own words:** explain the segment without the transcript, then compare.
- **Apply:** pose a new case and ask how the speaker's reasoning would carry over.
- Debrief teach-back covers the whole recording; the defense pack cites timestamps.
