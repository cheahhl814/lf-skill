# Medium adapter — documents (papers, protocols, SOPs, specs, manuals, docs)

Loaded by `SKILL.md` Phase 0 when the study target is written material. Loop, integrity
rules and ledger are unchanged. There is no "run", so questions anchor on the text.

## Intake

Get the full text (PDF, markdown, web page). Questions must quote or cite the actual
passage — section, page, table or figure number — never generic textbook questions.

## Objectives (derivation fallback)

- **Claim / purpose** — what the document says, or what the procedure achieves, in one
  sentence.
- **Method or procedure** — why each step exists and what a failure of it looks like.
- **Parameters and constraints** — every number, threshold, version or limit, and why
  it was chosen.
- **Evidence or output** — which figure, table, or expected result supports the claim
  or signals success.
- **Limits** — assumptions, scope, and what the document does not cover.

Tiering: protocols, SOPs, manuals and specs → steps, constraints and failure modes are
must-know. Papers → claim, design and statistics are must-know; discussion detail is
good-to-know.

## Anchors (checkpoints)

- Section boundaries (one checkpoint per major section, ≤ 6 per session).
- Before moving past a table, figure or procedure step with a real number in it.

## Evidence of understanding

- **Justify:** "why this threshold / control / step?" anchored to the passage.
- **Interpret:** show a real excerpt (figure, table row, spec clause) and ask what it
  supports or requires.
- **Apply:** pose a new case the document does not cover and ask what the student would do.
- **Critique (papers):** what would change the conclusion? what is the weakest design choice?
